Book of Confessions
We heartily give thanks to God for the confessional statements handed down through Church history which testify to the faithfulness of the saints who sought to carefully articulate the precious truths of the Bible, and we believe they are worthy of our study. We give special attention to those statements and their sections that most enable true Christian fellowship in light of our greatest and most trustworthy truths, namely, the person and work of the Lord Jesus Christ and his supremacy as Head over the body. We therefore subscribe to the following, holding them to be a faithful witness to what the Scriptures teach, and as a means of identifying with the broader Church: the Apostles’ Creed and the Chalecedionian Creed for their beautiful, simple, and nuanced articulation of the Triune God and the glory of the Triune God manifest in the person of Jesus; and the Spirit of the Reformation in the Five Solas and semper reformanda, which carry on the Prophetic and Apostolic tradition.
