Psalm 35
Tune: BRADBURY
Example: He Leadeth Me
1 Plead, Yahweh ‘gainst contending foes,
And fight with them who fight with me.
2 Take hold of buckler and of shield;
Rise up and my defender be.
3 Draw out the spear and stop the way
Against the men pursuing me;
And to my soul in mercy say,
"I am salvation unto Thee.”
BREAK
4 Let those who seek to take my soul
Themselves be humbled, shamed of face;
Let them be thwarted and turned back
Who are devising my disgrace.
5 Let them be chaff before the wind,
Yahweh’s angel is driving them;
All dark and slipp'ry be their way,
Yahweh’s angel is chasing them.
BREAK
9 My soul shall in Yahweh rejoice,
In His salvation boastful be;
10 Exulting, all my bones will say,
"Yahweh, who is like to Thee?
For Thou the poor deliverest
From one who is for him too strong;
The poor and needy Thou dost spare
From one who'd rob or do him wrong."
BREAK
16 As godless jesters at a feast
They with their teeth have gnashed at me.
17 How long, O Lord, wilt Thou look on?
Wilt Thou unheeding all this see?
From their destruction pluck my soul,
And snatch my life from lions strong.
18 Then with the saints I'll give Thee thanks.
And praise Thee in the mighty throng.
BREAK
22 Yahweh, Thou hast seen; be not still!
O Lord, be not far from my sight!
23 Stir up Thyself! To justice wake!
My God, my Lord, uphold my right!
24 Judge me justly, Yahweh my God,
And let them not rejoice in me,
25 Nor say in heart, "He is devoured!
Behold, our soul's desire we see!"
BREAK
26 Let them be shamed and humbled all
Who joy at my calamity;
Let them be clothed with shame, disgrace,
Who magnify themselves o'er me.
27 But let them shout and loud rejoice
Who long to see me justified;
And let them say with ceaseless voice,
“Yahweh be ever magnified!
BREAK
Because He loves His servant's peace!"
28 And thus my tongue will meditate
Upon Thy perfect righteousness,
And all the day Thy praise relate.
