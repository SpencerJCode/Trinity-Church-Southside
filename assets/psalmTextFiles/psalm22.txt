Psalm 22
Tune: CLEANSING FOUNTAIN (CMD)
Example: "There is a Fountain"
1 My God, my God, O why have You
Forsaken me? O why
Are You so far from giving help
And from my groaning cry?
2 By day and night, my God, I call;
Your answer still delays.
3 And yet You are the Holy One
Who dwells in Isr’el’s praise.
BREAK
4 Our fathers put their trust in You;
From You their rescue came.
5 They begged You and You set them free;
They were not put to shame.
6 But as for me, I am a worm
And not a man at all.
To men I am despised and base;
Their scornings on me fall.
BREAK
7 All those who look at me will laugh
And cast reproach at me.
Their mouths they open wide; they wag
Their heads in mockery.
8 “Yahweh was his reliance once;
Now see what God will send.
Yes, let God rise and set him free,
This man that was His friend.”
BREAK
9 You took me from my mother’s womb
To safety at the breast.
10 Since birth when I was cast on You
In You, my God, I rest.
11 Be not far off, for grief is near,
And none to help is found;
12 For bulls of Bashan in their strength
Now circle me around.
BREAK
13 Their lion jaws they open wide,
And roar to tear their prey.
14 My heart is wax, my bones unknit,
My life is poured away.
15 My strength is only broken clay;
My mouth and tongue are dry,
For in the very dust of death
You there make me to lie.
BREAK
16 For see how dogs encircle me!
On every side their stands
A brotherhood of cruelty;
They pierce my feet and hands.
17 My bones are plain for me to count;
Men see me and they stare.
18 My clothes among them they divide,
And gamble for their share.
BREAK
19 Now hurry, O my Strength, to help!
Do not be far, Yahweh!
20 But snatch my soul from raging dogs,
And spare me from the sword.
21 For lion’s mouth and oxen’s horns
O save me; hear my prayer!
22 And to my brethren in the church
Your name I will declare.
BREAK
23 Let those that fear Yahweh sing praise!
Give glory to Him now,
All Jacob’s seed; all Isr’el’s seed,
In awe before Him bow.
24 For He did not despise nor spurn
The grief of one oppressed,
Nor did He shun his cry for help,
But heard and gave him rest.
BREAK
25 When I proclaim my praise of You,
Then all the church will hear,
And I will pay my vows in full
Where men hold Him in fear.
26 The wretched poor will eat their fill
And will be made secure.
And those who seek will praise Yahweh.
So let your hearts endure.
BREAK
27 Then men remember will Yahweh
To earth’s remotest shore,
And all the Gentile kindreds turn
To worship and adore.
28 For all dominion in the earth
Is only of Yahweh
Among the nations He controls
The power of the sword.
BREAK
29 To Him will all the rich bow down
Who feast and live in ease.
And all those souls descend to dust
Will fall upon their knees.
30 There shall forever be a seed
To serve Him faithfully;
A generation of Yahweh
It shall accounted be.
BREAK
31 And they will come and will make known
To people yet to be
The righteousness that is His own,
For none did this but He
