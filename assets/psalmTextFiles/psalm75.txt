Psalm 75
Tune: ST COLUMBA CM
Example" “The King of Love My Shepherd Is”
1 To Thee, O God, we render thanks,
To Thee give thanks sincere,
Because Thy wondrous works declare
That Thy great name is near.
BREAK
2 When my appointed time is come,
I’ll judge with even hand.
3 Though earth and all its dwellers melt,
I make its pillars stand.
BREAK
4 I to the boastful said, "Boast not!"
To vile men, "Lift no horn!
5 Do not lift up your horn on high,
Nor speak with neck of scorn!"
BREAK
6 For not from east nor west nor wilds
Comes exaltation nigh,
7 For God is judge, debasing one,
Another raising high.
BREAK
8 Yahweh pours out a foaming cup
Which well-mixed wine contains,
And every wicked one on earth
Must drink; the dregs he drains.
BREAK
9 But I will tell it evermore,
To Jacob's God sing praise;
10 And horns of sinners I'll cut off,
But just men's horns I'll raise.
