Psalm 34
Tune: TOULON 10.10.10.10.
Example: “I Greet Thee, Who My Sure Redeemer Art”
1 All of my days I’ll bless and praise Yahweh;
His praise will ever be within my mouth.
2 Boast, O my soul, make your boast in Yahweh;
Let all the humble hear it and be glad.
BREAK
3 Come, join with me to magnify Yahweh.
Let us together praise His name on high!
4 Daily I sought; Yahweh has answered me,
And He from all my terrors set me free.
BREAK
5 Each one who looks to Him is not ashamed.
6 For this poor man called; Yahweh heard and saved.
7 Gathering armies ‘round those who fear Him
The Angel of Yahweh to rescue them
BREAK
8 Come, taste and see that Yahweh He is good;
Bless’d is the man who trusts in Him alone.
9 In fear of Yahweh, you saints ever dwell;
For those who fear God never suffer want.
BREAK
10 Join not the lions hungering for food,
But trust Yahweh instead, and have no want.
11 Know, children, come and listen unto me,
And I will teach you how to fear Yahweh.
BREAK
12 Love you long life and want to see good days?
13 May your tongue keep from evil and all lies!
14 Now from all evil flee—instead do good;
Seek peace and strive for it with heart and mind.
BREAK
19 Under His care, the righteous still have trials;
Very sure is Yahweh’s deliverance.
20 Well He protects their bones and does assure
not one of them will ever broken be.
BREAK
21 Expect that evil will slay wicked ones;
Yes, foes of righteous ones will be condemned.
22 Zion’s true servants, Yahweh does redeem;
None who take refuge in Him stand condemned.
