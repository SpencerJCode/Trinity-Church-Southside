Psalm 84
TUNE: AURELIA 76.76.D
EXAMPLE: “The Church’s One Foundation”
1 Yahweh of hosts, how lovely the place where Thou dost dwell.
Thy tabernacles holy in pleasantness excel.
2 My soul is longing, fainting, for Yahweh's courts to see;
My heart and flesh are crying, O living God, for Thee.
BREAK
3 Behold the sparrow findeth a house in which to rest,
The swallow has discovered where she may build her nest;
And where, securely sheltered, her young she forth may bring;
Yahweh of hosts, Thy altars I seek, my God and King.
BREAK
4 Blest who Thy house inhabit, they ever give Thee praise;
5 Blest all whom Thou dost strengthen, who love the sacred ways.
6 Who pass through Baca's valley, and make in it a well;
There rains in show’r abundant the pools with water fill.
BREAK
7 So they from strength unwearied go forward unto strength,
Till they appear in Zion, before our God at length.
8 Hear, Yahweh God of Jacob, to me an answer yield;
9 The face of Thy Anointed, behold, O God, our Shield.
BREAK
10 One day excels a thousand, if spent Thy courts within;
I’ll choose a threshold rather than dwell in tents of sin.
11 Our sun and shield o Yahweh, will grace and glory give;
No good will He deny them that uprightly do live.
BREAK
12 O Yahweh, God of armies how happy is the one
Who confidence reposes on Thee, Yahweh, alone.
