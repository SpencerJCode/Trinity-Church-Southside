PSALM 32
Tune: NETTLETON 87.87.D
Example: “Come Thou Fount of Every Blessing”
1 O how happy is the man whose
guilt has all forgiven been
Whose transgressions fully pardoned
And is covered all his sin.
2 Happy is the man whom Yahweh
Does not count iniquity
And within whose spirit there is
No deceit or treachery
BREAK
3 When I kept silent, my bones aged
And my groaning filled each day.
4 Every day Your hand oppressed me
Scorched my strength, dried it away.
5 Then I made known my sin to you,
Hid not from my guiltiness;
I said, “I will before Yahweh
All transgressions now confess.”
BREAK
Then my sin You did forgive it
And You took my guilt away.
6 For this, when You near at hand are
Then let all the godly pray.
Safe from all the rising waters.
7 You alone my hiding place
And You will now comfort me with
Songs of victory and of grace.
BREAK
8 I will give to you Instruction,
And I’ll teach you as you go.
With my eye I’ll guide your steps and
By my counsel you will know.
9 Be no longer dumb like horse or mule
But endeavor to subdue
Use the bit and use the bridle
Lest he never come to you.
BREAK
10 Many are the pains of evil
But Thy steadfast loyal love
Wraps around all those whose trust is
Only in Yahweh above.
11 O ye righteous cry aloud with glee
And in Yahweh now rejoice!
All the just and true of heart to
Sing and shout with joyful voice.
