PSALM 30
Tune: LANCASHIRE 76.76.D
Example: “LEAD ON, O KING ETERNAL”
1 Yahweh, I will exalt You,
For You have lifted me;
My foes You have allowed not
To glory over me.
2 Yahweh my God, I pleaded
That You might heal and save;
3 Yahweh, from death have ransomed
And kept me from the grave.
BREAK
4 His saints, O praise you Yahweh
And thank His holy name.
5 His anger lasts a moment,
His favor is the same
For sorrow, like a pilgrim,
May tarry all the night,
But then a shout of joy comes
When dawns the morning light.
BREAK
6 In prosp’rous days I boasted,
“Unmoved I shall remain.”
7 Yahweh, You by Your favor
My mount in strength maintain;
For when Your face was hidden,
I soon was trouble sore.
8 I’ll cry to You, O Yahweh;
Yahweh I will implore.
BREAK
9 Is there in my blood profit
When in the grave I dwell?
Will dust proclaim Your praises,
Your truth and glory tell?
10 Now hear my voice, O Yahweh!
Be gracious unto me!
To You I cry, O Yahweh!
O now my helper be!
BREAK
11 You now have turned my sorrow
To dancing full of joy;
You loosened all my sackcloth
And girded me with joy.
12 That I may sing praise to You,
And never silent be!
Yahweh my God, I’ll thank You
Through all eternity.
