PSALM 147
Tune: FOREST GREEN
Example: "HO! YE THAT THIRST"
1. O praise Yahweh—how good it is to sing praise to our God;
How pleasant and how fitting when we sing His praise abroad!
2 Yahweh builds up Jerusalem, and gathers those outcast;
3 He heals the brokenhearted, and binds up their wounds at last.
BREAK
4 He counts the number of the stars, and calls them each by name.
5 Great is Yahweh in pow’r and might—our minds can’t grasp His fame.
6 Yahweh sustains the humble, and supports the ones bowed down,
But stands against the wicked ones, and casts them to the ground.
BREAK
7 Sing to Yahweh with thanks and praise; with harp, make music – sing!
8 He covers heaven with His clouds; to earth His rain does bring.
He makes the grass grow on the hills, provides the beasts with food;
9 Young ravens, too, which cry to Him—supplies them all with food.
BREAK
10 His pleasure’s not in horse’s strength, nor in the legs of man;
11 Yahweh delights in those who fear, who trust His loving plan.
12 Extol Yahweh, Jerusalem, and praise your God, Zi-on!
13 He strengthened all your bars and gates—within you bless’d your sons.
BREAK
14 The Lord grants peace within your walls; with wheat He satisfies.
15 He sends forth His command on earth; His word runs swift and flies.
16 He spreads the snow like wool around, and scatters frost like ash.
17 He hurls His hail like pebbles—who can stand His icy blast?
BREAK
18 He sends His word and so melts them; stirs wind so waters flow;
19 Declares His words to Jacob and His laws for them to know.
20 No other nation has received His laws or known His ways;
He’s done this all for Is-ra-el; O to Yahweh be praise!
