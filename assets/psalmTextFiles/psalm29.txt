Psalm 29
Tune: ASH GROVE 12.11.12.11
Example: “Let All Things Now Living”
1 Ascribe to Yahweh, you sons of the Almighty,
Both glory and strength attribute to Yahweh!
2 Ascribe to Yahweh His name’s greatness of glory!
You sons, worship Yahweh in holy array
3 The voice of Yahweh is over the waters,
The glorious God thunders forth from the height.
Yahweh is upon the great sweep of the waters
4 Yahweh’s voice in splendor! Yahweh’s voice in might!
BREAK
5 The voice of Yahweh is shattering the cedars!
Yahweh splinters Lebanon’s cedars apart!
6 The slopes of Mount Hermon like calves they are leaping!
And Lebanon’s hills like young antelope start!
7 The voice of Yahweh divides the flames of lightning
And causes the fiery flashes to break!
8 The voice of Yahweh makes the wilderness tremble;
Yahweh makes the desert of Kadesh to shake!
BREAK
9 The voice of Yahweh makes the deer twist in labor!
The high standing forest of trees it strips bare!
And all of the length and the breadth of His temple
And all things within it His glory declare!
10 Yahweh on His throne sat above the great deluge!
Yahweh on His throne sits as King without cease!
11 Yahweh is the One Who gives strength to His people!
Yahweh is the One Who will bless them with peace!
